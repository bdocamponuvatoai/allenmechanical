## Content decisions

- The CEO title and 35+ years statement are supplied by the user.
- Four capability groups are a presentation of the supplied mechanical/HVAC, plumbing/heating and fabrication services, not an invented count of operating divisions.
- Service scope lists are explicitly discussion prompts; materials, exact work, availability, timing and prices are confirmed directly.
- No invented case studies, named clients, reviews, trade certifications, engineering qualifications, emergency response promises, performance metrics, warranties or manufacturer partnerships.
- The site does not claim that generated images represent actual company work or actual staff.
- No published certification details are reproduced: scope and expiry may change and are unnecessary for this preview.
